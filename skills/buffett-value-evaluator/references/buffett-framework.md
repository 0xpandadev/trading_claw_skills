# Buffett Fit Framework

Score range: 0-100

## Component Weights

1. Moat Durability (0-25)
2. Management Quality And Capital Allocation (0-20)
3. Owner Earnings Quality (0-20)
4. Financial Safety (0-15)
5. Valuation And Margin Of Safety (0-20)

## 1) Moat Durability (0-25)

Check:

- pricing power evidence
- retention and customer lock-in
- scale, brand, network, or switching-cost advantages

## 2) Management And Capital Allocation (0-20)

Check:

- reinvestment discipline
- buyback quality (price-sensitive, not mechanical)
- acquisition discipline and post-deal returns
- transparency and shareholder alignment

## 3) Owner Earnings Quality (0-20)

Check:

- cash conversion quality
- maintenance vs growth capex understanding
- normalized earnings stability

## 4) Financial Safety (0-15)

Check:

- debt service resilience
- refinancing dependence
- stress-case survival characteristics

## 5) Valuation And Margin Of Safety (0-20)

Check:

- conservative intrinsic value range
- current price discount to conservative value
- downside asymmetry

## Score Bands

- 85-100: core candidate
- 70-84: high-quality watchlist
- 55-69: fair business, valuation-sensitive
- below 55: pass unless special catalyst

## Decision Labels

- `Own`: quality + valuation aligned
- `Watch`: quality good, valuation not yet attractive
- `Pass`: quality or safety gap
