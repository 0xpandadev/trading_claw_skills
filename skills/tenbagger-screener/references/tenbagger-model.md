# Tenbagger Score Model

Score range: 0-100

## Component Weights

1. Growth Runway (0-25)
2. Reinvestment Engine (0-20)
3. Business Quality And Durability (0-20)
4. Financial Strength (0-15)
5. Valuation Setup (0-10)
6. Catalyst And Timing (0-10)

## 1) Growth Runway (0-25)

Check:

- revenue CAGR trend and acceleration quality
- TAM headroom and market share trajectory
- evidence of multi-year demand drivers

Guide:

- 20-25: large runway with execution proof
- 12-19: decent runway with partial proof
- 0-11: limited or unclear runway

## 2) Reinvestment Engine (0-20)

Check:

- reinvestment rate and returns on incremental capital
- product expansion, distribution expansion, margin path
- unit economics coherence

Guide:

- 16-20: reinvestment converts to durable growth
- 10-15: mixed but positive
- 0-9: weak reinvestment productivity

## 3) Business Quality And Durability (0-20)

Check:

- pricing power
- customer retention and switching costs
- competitive moat signals

Guide:

- 16-20: strong moat trajectory
- 10-15: moderate moat
- 0-9: weak defensibility

## 4) Financial Strength (0-15)

Check:

- leverage and liquidity
- cash burn quality and runway
- dilution risk

Guide:

- 12-15: strong balance sheet
- 7-11: manageable
- 0-6: fragile

## 5) Valuation Setup (0-10)

Check:

- valuation relative to growth quality and durability
- downside protection vs upside convexity

Guide:

- 8-10: attractive asymmetry
- 4-7: fair
- 0-3: expensive or poor asymmetry

## 6) Catalyst And Timing (0-10)

Check:

- identifiable 3-12 month re-rating triggers
- event quality: earnings inflection, product cycle, margin turn, policy shift

Guide:

- 8-10: clear catalyst path
- 4-7: partial visibility
- 0-3: weak trigger visibility

## Score Bands

- 85-100: priority deep-dive
- 70-84: watchlist with focused diligence
- 55-69: optional tracking
- below 55: no action unless thesis changes

## Scenario Overlay

For top candidates, produce:

- bull case expected value range
- base case expected value range
- bear case drawdown profile
- key signposts for scenario switching
